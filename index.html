Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Knvrt ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in privacy by design. Knvrt does not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or payment credentials.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All user preferences, custom currency selections, pinned favorites, calculator history, and active settings are stored locally on your device storage. This data never leaves your device unless backed up by your operating system's standard backup mechanisms.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To improve app reliability, stability, and performance, the App integrates standard third-party services provided by Google. These services may collect certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic information:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): Collects crash reports, stack traces, and device performance metrics to help us fix bugs. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable analytics data regarding app usage patterns to help us enhance user experience. Google Privacy Policy
  • Currency Exchange Rates Providers: The app connects via secure HTTPS to fetch up-to-date foreign exchange rates. No user identifiers or personal data are transmitted during these requests.

4. Device Permissions

Knvrt requests only the minimal permissions necessary for core operation:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to download current currency exchange rates and send anonymous crash reports.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to provide optional haptic feedback when pressing keypad buttons.

5. Children's Privacy

Knvrt does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Security

All network communications (currency rate updates and diagnostic reports) are performed exclusively over industry-standard encrypted channels (HTTPS / TLS).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
